The FootprintIridium · Inmarsat · Thuraya

Iridium's 66-satellite low-Earth-orbit constellation covers the entire planet — every ocean, every desert, every pole. Inmarsat's three geostationary satellites cover everything between roughly 76°N and 76°S. Thuraya covers the EMEA + Central Asia + Australia region.

⌖ Iridium: 66 LEO Sats · ⌖ Inmarsat: 3 GEO · ⌖ Thuraya: 2 GEO
